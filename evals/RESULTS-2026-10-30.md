## Against the criteria fixed beforehand

- Score with the skill at least 0.85 and a delta of at least 0.25: met (0.93, delta 0.34).
- Cost ratio at most 1.5 (goal 1.3): **not met**, 1.71 (it was 1.8 on 2026-10-26). The skill was not tuned further after this.

## Reading

- The gain is in process: naming a missing tool (S2), backing up (S5), staying in code-only mode (S3), applying a staged plan (S11), citing the Spec's tolerance (S6). Where the agent without the skill already does the work (S1, S4, S7) there is no score gain and the skill only adds cost.
- Finding the underlying difference does not depend on the skill: both arms found the causes in S6. The skill adds the accounting and the approval path around it.
- S12 lost its advantage (0.50 and 0.50; it was 0.70 and 0.40). I have not read the runs to see whether the skill or the judge is at fault. S2 fell from 1.00 to 0.75.
- Cost is driven by turns (writing files, following the cycle), not by the size of `SKILL.md`. A 43-word trim had no measurable effect.

## Limits

Two runs per arm; the skill and the scenarios were tuned together; one model and one agent; synthetic data only. S9–S11 were rerun separately because their setup needs sqlglot, which the first launch lacked.

## The invalid first attempt of this day

The first runs of 2026-10-30 (S6, S12, S14 to S17) were launched without `--allow-tools Write Edit Bash`. The agents could not run scripts or write files, so turns, costs and scores from that attempt (including a claimed "4.9x" ratio for S6) mean nothing and are not used anywhere.
